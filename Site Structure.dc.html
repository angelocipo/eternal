02  Collection

/collection.html
  1. Page header with the piece count
  2. Filter row — type, stone, price band, in stock; sort by newest or price
  3. Product grid — photograph, name, stone, price, hover state
  4. Empty state when the filters match nothing
  5. Cross-link into the Compendium for anyone browsing by meaning rather than by piece

Runs off one product data file, so a new piece is a single entry, not a new page.

03  Product page

/product.html?id=…

Section order follows the competitor's structure, with the stone detail carried further.

  1. Gallery — four to six photographs, thumbnails at the side, on-wrist shot included
  2. Buy block — name, stone, price, wrist size selector, quantity, add to cart, stock line
  3. Assurances — hand-assembled in Rome, tracked shipping, returns window
  4. Description — two short paragraphs in your voice
  5. Specification table — stone, bead diameter, hardness, origin, metal, clasp, length, weight
  6. The stone — its meaning and properties, linked to its Compendium entry
  7. How it is made — three or four steps of the actual process, with photographs
  8. Care — how to keep the stone and the silver right
  9. Shipping and returns — times and costs, collapsible
  10. Related pieces — four, same stone or same family
